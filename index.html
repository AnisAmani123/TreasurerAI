GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Do I need accounting experience?

No. The workbook uses plain labels, beginner instructions and formulas for totals. You still need to enter figures accurately and review the results.

Does it connect to my bank automatically?

No. For privacy and control, it does not access your bank account. You paste or enter statement transactions, assign categories and check the totals against the actual statement.

Will it automatically understand my bank statement?

Not in this version. It is a manual-entry/paste workbook, not an AI statement-reading service. Review every entry for accuracy.

Can I use it for another reunion?

Yes. Save a fresh copy, update the reunion details and member list, and retain the previous workbook as your record for the earlier reunion.

What software do I need?

It is an Excel workbook (.xlsx). Microsoft Excel is recommended. Other spreadsheet applications may display or calculate some formulas differently, so test your chosen app before relying on it for committee reporting.

Is this official accounting or tax advice?

No. It is a practical record-keeping template, not accounting, legal or tax advice. Keep your bank statements and receipts, and have the committee review the accounts.
