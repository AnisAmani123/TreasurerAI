OWNER SETUP GUIDE

Get your website ready to launch.

The website files are built. The final launch step is connecting a checkout provider that can pay out to you in the Cook Islands.

1

Check payment eligibility first

Check this before building your store around a particular platform. PayPal's official country table currently shows Cook Islands as send-only, and Payhip requires sellers to connect a supported payment processor. That means you should not assume PayPal/Stripe or a digital-download platform can pay you out in the Cook Islands. Confirm with the provider using your real seller location and bank account before taking orders.

PayPal country support table →   Payhip payout requirements →

2

Upload the product

Upload Family_Reunion_Treasurer_Workbook.xlsx as the paid digital product. Set your product title, price, customer receipt and download delivery in that platform.

3

Copy the secure checkout link

Copy your product checkout URL from the seller platform. This is the link customers should use to pay and access their download after successful checkout.

4

Connect the button

Open config.js in a text editor and replace the blank checkoutUrl with the URL in double quotes. Change the price and currency there if needed, save, and test the page.

Before going live

  • Complete a test visit on a desktop and phone.
  • Confirm the checkout link opens the right product.
  • Verify the platform can pay out to your eligible bank/payment account.
  • Confirm the customer receives the workbook after payment.
  • Add your support email in config.js or add it to the page footer.
  • Review the final selling price, refund wording and privacy details.

Publish the website

For a simple static website, GitHub Pages can publish HTML/CSS/JavaScript from a repository and supports a free GitHub-hosted address or a custom domain. Follow the official guide linked below. Do not upload the paid workbook into the public website folder unless you intend everyone to download it for free.

Read the GitHub Pages overview →

Important: The included site is a front-end website, not a registered online store. The checkout button intentionally stays unconnected until you provide your own verified product checkout link. This avoids collecting or handling customers' card details on this simple website.
